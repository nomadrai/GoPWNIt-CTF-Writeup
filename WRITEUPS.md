## Reading the records

- **Saved** means a flag is present in a pre-existing writeup, extracted output,
  or another local artifact. It does not independently assert scoreboard acceptance;
  individual writeups describe the evidence available.
- **Submission note** means the exact final flag or key was not retained locally.
  These writeups include the participant-provided note that it was not saved at
  the moment of submission due to hurry, followed by a clearly labeled probable
  approach based on existing scripts or the published description.
- This handoff was assembled by reviewing existing material. No challenge
  solvers, key searches, decryptions, or new flag submissions were executed.
